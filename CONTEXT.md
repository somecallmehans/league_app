# League App

Shared language for the Magic: the Gathering league product: sessions, rounds, pods, participants, and decklists.

## Language

### League night

**Aversion**:
A global, undirected pairing of two Participants who should not share a Pod.
_Avoid_: Avoid, blacklist, block, do-not-pod, pairing preference

**Open**:
A Pod that is not Submitted.
_Avoid_: unlocked, editable

**Participant**:
A player who can sign in to league sessions and sit in pods.

**Participation**:
Points a Participant receives for a Round by sitting in a Pod when that Pod first becomes Submitted.
_Avoid_: attendance, round-open points

**Pod**:
A seating of 3, 4, or 5 Participants within a Round.
_Avoid_: table, group, flight

**Completed**:
A Round in which every Pod is Submitted.

**Roll call**:
The physical attendance check at a store, after no-shows are taken off Sign-in and before a Round is Started. The product does not record it.

**Round**:
One of two seating/scoring periods within a Session (Round 1 or Round 2).

**Sign-in**:
A Participant's place on a Round before that Round is Started.
_Avoid_: roll call, check-in

**Started**:
A Round that has Pods.
_Avoid_: begun

**Submitted**:
A Pod whose scores have been turned in. A Submitted Pod's membership is fixed.
_Avoid_: locked, closed, final

**Session**:
A single league night at a store.

### Decklists

**Decklist**:
A named list a Participant submits, with a commander, an optional partner or background, and an optional companion.
_Avoid_: Deck

**Color identity**:
The colors of a Decklist's commander combined with its partner or background. A companion stands outside that identity.
_Avoid_: Deck colors

**Named credit**:
A Decklist's choice to display its Participant's name. The choice belongs to that Decklist alone.
_Avoid_: give_credit, attribution

**Win**:
A pod victory. It may be linked to the Decklist that was played.
_Avoid_: Match win, game win

**Deckbuilding achievement**:
An achievement attached to a Decklist that the Decklist can earn when it wins.
_Avoid_: Earned achievement, potential achievement
